FHA, VA and USDA loans are assumable. Conventional loans are not. So the odds of finding a seller with a sub-4% mortgage you can take over are mostly a function of geography.

Government-backed share of home-purchase originations, 2019-21 (HMDA):

Alaska — 39.4%
Mississippi, New Mexico, Oklahoma — 30.5%
Louisiana — 29.9%
Alabama — 29.4%

Massachusetts — 9.9%
Wisconsin — 10.5%
New York — 11.1%

A buyer in Oklahoma is roughly three times as likely to find an assumable note as one in Massachusetts.
