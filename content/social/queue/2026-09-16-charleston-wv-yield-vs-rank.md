Charleston, WV has the highest gross rental yield of any metro we track — 10.1% on a typical $150,171 home renting for $1,264/mo (July 2026 Zillow data). It is also the cheapest entry of the 165 markets.

It ranks #4, not #1.

Past 8% gross yield, more yield stops moving the Star Score — the yield factor tops out. Charleston's 10.1% and #1 Florence's 8.6% both max that factor. The gap is everywhere else: equity growth 48 vs 58, landlord-friendliness 75 vs 85.

Screening on yield alone stops telling you things at 8%.
