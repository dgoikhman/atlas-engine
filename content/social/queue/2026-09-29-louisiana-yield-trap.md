Five of the 11 highest gross rental yields among the 242 US metros we track are in Louisiana (August 2026 Zillow data):

Shreveport — 8.6%
Houma — 8.5%
Monroe — 8.2%
Lake Charles — 8.2%

None of them ranks higher than #28 on the Star Score.

Two reasons. Houma has the weakest 5-year home-value appreciation of all 242 metros, and Lake Charles is in the bottom three. And our climate & insurance score for Louisiana is 25/100, the lowest of any state.

That yield is the price of the risk.
